Implementation note:
- Static ASL model has 24 classes: A–Y excluding J and Z.
- SignBridge reports 94.7% held-out validation accuracy for this model.
- The browser feature vector is 63 values: 21 MediaPipe hand landmarks × XYZ,
  normalized by wrist origin, wrist-to-middle-MCP scale, and handedness mirroring.
