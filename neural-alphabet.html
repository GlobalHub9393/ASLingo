What we're testing

Make a letter naturally. Don't twist your hand until the app agrees. We want to know whether a trained model handles your normal E, K, O, P, R, X and the A/S/T/M/N family better than our hand-written rules.

J and Z are intentionally excluded. They are motion letters and need a temporal recognizer. This page is a static-handshape test first.
